## Third-party material

Third-party scientific data, licenses and other assets retain their own terms.
Their source, checksum and known limitations are recorded in
`ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` and package-level manifests. Inclusion in this repository
does not place them under GPLv2.

In particular, the PyBDSF replay package preserves the upstream repository's
GPLv3 license, and the casacore Measurement Set replay package preserves its
upstream LGPLv2 license. These separately identified sample packages are
distributed with their copied license texts and do not change the root
project's GPLv2 terms.

Contributors should record provenance when adding external material and should
not assume that attribution alone grants redistribution rights.
